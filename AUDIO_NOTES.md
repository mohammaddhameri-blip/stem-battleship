# STEAM Battleship — Audio notes

The background music and sound effects are original, procedurally synthesised using the Web Audio API in [audio-engine.js](audio-engine.js).

- **Soundtrack:** "STEAM Battle Theme," an original 128 BPM, 16-bar repeating minor-key action score.
- **Sound effects:** Original synthesised cannon, Flak, torpedo, B-2 flyover, incoming missile, explosion, sinking, repair, radio, movement, correct/wrong answer, and victory cues.
- **Third-party samples or music files:** None.
- **Internet connection required for audio:** No.
- **Royalty fees or required third-party attribution for this built-in audio:** None.
- **Implementation:** Audio is synthesised locally in the browser, and can be played as part of this game without purchasing commercial music or paying royalties.

The player can independently enable or disable the soundtrack and sound effects and control their volumes. Saved preferences stay in their browser. Playback starts after a user gesture when a game is launched, in line with mobile/browser autoplay policies.

This statement covers the original audio implementation, not third-party components of the wider game.
