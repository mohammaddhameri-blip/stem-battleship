HOW TO PLAY
1Answer one four-choice question.
2Adjust aim, choose ammunition, then fire once.
3Repeat for each shot. Wrong answers use a random firing angle.

Host chooses Auto Roam or Full Manual (one question per action). Ships stay in their team's half. Flak fires three rounds at 5 damage each. Repair restores 5 HP and ends the turn.

The FIT button shows the entire board. Host controls online match positions. A collected radio unlocks a 35-damage airstrike.
