HOW TO PLAY
1ANSWER a question.
2TAP an adjacent square · AIM · EQUIP.
3FIRE or REPAIR. Either ends the attack cycle.

After an incoming shot resolves, defensive tile movement is free until the opponent fires again.

Wrong answers cause random shots. Wrong answers send shots in random directions. Repair restores 35 HP. Torpedo requires three consecutive correct answers.

Online: up to 3v3. When the other team misses five consecutive volleys, a radio appears on your side. Collect it to call a B-2 airstrike (35 damage). Tap an enemy to select a target.
